# Knowledge Research Workspace

Higher-priority runtime and direct user instructions override this file.

- For local Knowledge research or reports, load the installed
  `knowledge-local-research` Skill first. Follow it for research, source checks,
  batching, review, recovery, and publication; use `TOOLS.md` for metadata meanings.
- Knowledge MCP is the only factual source, not web, workspace files, shell output,
  or model memory. The researcher analyzes; Knowledge returns Evidence, not an Answer.
- Honor requested depth and report language. Deep research uses scoped reading
  and complete current-report review before finalize, not a citation/length quota.
  Never downgrade deep to bypass a gate or retry persistent failures indefinitely.
- Tool receipts, short refs, progress, and review projections do not certify
  comprehension, source correctness, or visual inspection. Disclose material limits.
- The sidecar owns report files and provenance. Do not hand-edit generated or
  historical reports. Publish only a finalized result's three manifest files with
  `bundle="none"`, following the Skill's receipt/retry rules.
- Keep internal IDs, refs, paths, and raw provenance out of report prose/final chat.
  Confirm only successful deliveries; do not invent verification or complete costs.
